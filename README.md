## Hi, I'm Suyu.

I'm a mathematics undergraduate at the **University of British Columbia**, interested in the mathematical foundations of machine learning.

**Research interests:** optimization, probability and statistical learning, and algorithm analysis.

I'm particularly interested in how assumptions about data and structure affect what an algorithm can learn or guarantee. My projects explore these questions through probabilistic models, graph-based optimization, and numerical methods.
